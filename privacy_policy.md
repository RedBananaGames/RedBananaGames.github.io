# Hoponaut: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2 November 2026*

This privacy policy explains how **Hoponaut** (the "game"), made by **Red Banana Games**
("we", "us"), handles information when you play it on Android devices.

## The short version

- You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or any other
  personal details.
- Your progress (scores, coins, unlocked characters, settings) is stored on your device.
  If you use **Google Play Games**, it's also backed up to your own Play Games account
  so you can continue on another phone. We never receive it.
- The game shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**: small banners on its menu screens,
  a full-screen ad between some runs, and optional ads you can watch for gems. AdMob may
  collect some device information to show and measure ads.
- The game has no in-app purchases.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in its own private storage on your device:
best scores and a list of your best runs, coins, gems, unlocked characters and
upgrades, and settings such as sound, music, tilt controls and language.

This information isn't sent to us. It is deleted from your device when you uninstall the
game; a copy stays in your Google Play Games account if you use Play Games (see below).

## Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game signs in to it
automatically (you can play without it). Through Google Play Games:

- your **game progress** is saved to your Play Games account ("Saved Games") so it
  comes back on a new phone or after reinstalling;
- your **scores** are submitted to the game's world leaderboard, shown with your Play
  Games name and avatar, according to your Play Games profile visibility settings.

This data is stored by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy, and is linked to
your Play Games profile, not to us. We don't receive your name, email or other account
details. You can delete your Play Games data for this game, or your whole profile, from
your Play Games profile (https://play.google.com/games/profile) or your Google Account.
Google also collects some diagnostics about the Play Games service itself.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show banner ads on its menu screens and an occasional
full-screen ad between runs (never during gameplay), and optional ads you can choose to
watch for in-game rewards. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process
information such as your device's advertising ID, device type and operating system,
general location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your
  consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can
  change your choice at any time from the game's settings.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your
  device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy →
  Ads, depending on your device).

How Google uses this information is explained in Google's own policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Children

Hoponaut is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Data sharing and selling

We don't collect personal information, so we don't sell or share any. The only third
parties involved are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play Games (sign-in, saved games and
the leaderboard), as described above.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
